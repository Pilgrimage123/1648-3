# Dormant Hero Special Abilities in Mission 1

Status: Accepted

Mission 1 leaves `ability` undefined on all player roster units so the `[5]` special-ability bar (`protector_oath`, `klementinum_verdict`, `maltese_shrapnel`, `marshal_command`) stays dormant in normal play, keeping early turns focused on musketry, segmented reloading, powder kegs, and escort positioning while preserving the full execution, VFX, calculation, and localization paths for future scenarios or unit definitions.
