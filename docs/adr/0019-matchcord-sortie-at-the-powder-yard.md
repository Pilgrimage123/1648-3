# The powder yard is a fuse, and the carts are the clock

Mission 2 closed the Old Town Bridge Tower on the morning of 26 July 1648 and left a later decision open: Swedish forces after that morning are not infernal unless a later decision says so. Historically the next morning, 27 July, Königsmarck began bombarding the right bank with guns taken from the castle armory. The city's own artillery had sat in that armory since 1547, so the Old Town had almost nothing to answer with. Mission 3, **Doutnák**, is the night of 27/28 July. Father Plachý and two others cross the Čertovka and fire the powder yard behind the Malá Strana bridge towers before that battery can be fed.

The win is the store cooking off with fewer than three kegs already through the battery gate, and Plachý himself on the sortie skiff afterwards. It is not a cleared yard. The new rule is one cord. Everything else is a rule the company already has: the powder keg, smoke, rain on a matchlock, high ground, a roofed room.

The cord is deterministic. Plachý alone lights it, rain does not drown it after that, and the spark moves two tiles at the end of the player turn, before the Swedes act. A same-round race therefore favors the spark. Randomness stays where a shot already is random: a damp matchlock in the open, and which of two doors the relief uses when the silence breaks. The guaranteed answer to a cruel percentage is the cord, not another roll.

The clock runs while the party is still unseen. Carts roll from the first Swedish turn. Silence is time to take positions, not time to stop the yard. Once silence breaks, the master gunner shows his canister cone for one Swedish turn and only then fires it. Half cover does not stop canister. A pillar or the lodge does. Sitting in the lane on overwatch is the play this field punishes.

This is that later decision. The Swedes here are a master gunner, bearers, and matchlock men. The occultist does not return. No one from Mission 1 stands on the field. The sortie is always three people: sworn survivors fill the places, and a Clementinum student fills each empty one, so a refused oath cannot skip the night.

## Considered Options

- **A numbered turn limit.** Rejected because the pressure belongs to the carts the player can see and interrupt. A banner that says the night has twelve turns would ask for the same hurry and explain nothing.
- **Winning by shooting the kegs, as in Mission 1.** Rejected because that night already taught the shot. A single keg is not the store. The store goes up only when the spark enters its door.
- **A second kneeling, in the manner of the tower inscription.** Rejected because Plachý's act on this field is to light the cord. A blessing that only announced a burn rate the spark already shows would be a copy of the rite.
- **Rain that may snuff the cord.** Rejected because the action the player commits must happen. Weather already has a place: outdoor matchlocks misfire. The waxed cord does not.
- **Craters that seal the way back to the skiff.** Rejected because a cooked tile stays walkable at its center. Debris sits on the edge. The walk back is dangerous from smoke, canister, and open ground, not from a blocked grid.
- **The Petřín powder magazine, the finished Vrtbovská zahrada, or a return to the Grand Priory.** Rejected because the Petřín magazine is a nineteenth-century building, the terraced garden is later baroque, and the Priory is the unplundered palace of Mission 1. This yard is a plundered coach house by the Malá Strana bridgehead.
- **Infernal harriers, or the Mission 1 survivor in the party.** Rejected because the bridge was the infernal field, and the survivor's death or life belongs to the chronicle. He may have named the water gate. He does not fight here.

## Consequences

The working rules live in `GLOSSARY.md` under Mise 3. The lane from store mouth to battery gate has to be short enough that waiting on the skiff loses, and long enough that a party which moves on the first turn can still stop a third delivery. The store door has to sit within a six-tile cord of a tail Plachý can reach, including on the night Ondřej is absent and the lodge is climbed by the stair. The cook-off scars the yard only. It does not reach the Čertovka.

No Mission 3 field is implemented yet. When it is, it should not invent a second explosive, a stealth attribute, or a UI fuse.
